# FIN-POL-06: Software Procurement & SaaS Vendor Review Policy

## 1. Competitive Bidding & Approval Thresholds
Policy `FIN-POL-06` standardizes how engineering and operations teams evaluate, purchase, and renew third-party software licenses, cloud tools, and API subscriptions.

### 1.1. Three-Quote Rule & Payment Terms
- Any new SaaS or software contract with an annualized value of **$5,000/year or above** (`>= $5,000/year`) requires at least **3 comparable vendor quotes** documented in the procurement ticket before Finance sign-off.
- Contracts below `$5,000/year` require Department Lead approval and a budget check against the quarterly engineering cost center.
- Standard corporate supplier payment terms are **Net-30 days** from receipt of a valid electronic invoice; auto-renewing annual subscriptions on personal corporate cards above `$500/month` are prohibited.

## 2. Third-Party Vendor Security Review
Before integrating any SaaS vendor or external API that processes `INTERNAL`, `CONFIDENTIAL`, or `RESTRICTED` corporate data, the requesting engineer must initiate a Vendor Security Assessment.

### 2.1. Required Security Attestations
The vendor must submit either a current **SOC 2 Type II** audit report or an active **ISO 27001** certification, complete the security questionnaire, and execute a Data Processing Agreement (**DPA**) specifying breach notification within 48 hours and data deletion upon contract termination.
